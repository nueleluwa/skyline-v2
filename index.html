More than an address, a declaration

There are developments that provide accommodation. And then there are developments that redefine the skyline of a city.

Rising within the prestigious Oniru district, two sculptural towers stand on Victoria Island’s coastal edge. Luxury residences, premium retail, leisure, hospitality and modern workspaces come together in a single architectural masterpiece, with the Atlantic flowing through its design.
